<summary>Mod Information</summary>

Void Ascension's plot is based around the idea of the void within Minecraft growing a sentient thought Process, through unknown Means at first. As you progress through the mod and adventure deep into the void, you will piece together the truth as to how the void became sentient and sought to destroy all living things.
